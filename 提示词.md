# 积水下的画面 · GPT 生图提示词

**怎么用**：每张图都生成 **1:1 正方形**（2048×2048 最好），保存到 `scenes/` 文件夹，按 `1.jpg`、`2.jpg`、`3.jpg`…… 编号（png / webp 也行）。网页会按编号顺序轮播，她每跑过约一屏就淡入下一张。

所有提示词共用同一段"风格尾巴"，保证换图时画风统一。下面每条 = 主体描述 + 风格尾巴。

## 风格尾巴（每条都要加在后面）
```
Top-down view looking straight down into a shallow pool of still rainwater at night, the figure appears submerged beneath the water surface, slightly blurred and refracted by gentle ripples. Hand-painted gouache and acrylic texture, visible brush strokes, classic 1990s hand-drawn Japanese animation background art style. Deep midnight blue and teal palette, tiny glowing star-like specks floating in the water, soft cyan glow around the figure. Center of the image is the clearest, edges fade into dark blue water. Cinematic, dreamy, quiet, high detail, no text, no watermark, no frame, square 1:1.
```
